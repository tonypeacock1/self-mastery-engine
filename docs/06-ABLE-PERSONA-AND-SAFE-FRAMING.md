## 3. HelpAnxiety topic list — SAFE vs UNSAFE for ranking sites

### SAFE to adapt (emotional / SA stress / performance language)
- Load-shedding stress / can’t switch off under ambient crisis
- Financial pressure + work load (trapped stress, can’t quit)
- High-functioning emptiness / successful but hollow
- Crime-related hypervigilance expressed as stress (not PTSD diagnosis pages)
- “Suck it up” male culture → private composure failure
- Retrenchment / career shock as stress reaction (not clinical depression pages)
- Relationship / people-load burnout
- Parent rage / short fuse under pressure
- Meaning beyond material success

### UNSAFE for ranking PSL (keep off public mini-sites)
- Anxiety/depression/bipolar/ADHD/OCD/eating-disorder diagnosis or treatment pages
- Medication side-effect lists, “dangers of antidepressants”
- Psychiatry vs Dianetics attack pages
- Sangoma vs reactive mind comparative medical claims
- “Dianetics ends depression” type claims
- PTSD / farm attacks / trauma as clinical products
- Substance abuse as clinical treatment offer

**Reason:** YMYL risk, Google quality, and the book-as-filter works better when the reader is able and seeking a tool — not when the page is competing as a medical alternative.

Internal / operator education can still reference the full list; **ranking HTML must not**.
