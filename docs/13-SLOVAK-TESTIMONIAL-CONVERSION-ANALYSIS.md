## Why this matters for South Africa

The Slovakia page is a **working book-as-filter funnel**. Buyers self-select. Testimonials show:

1. People search for **understanding** (self, others, life stuck points) — not clinical diagnosis.
2. The book is positioned as **answers + mechanism + something to apply**.
3. Social proof is raw Facebook comments (ordered / reading / changed life) — high trust.
4. Price was framed as accessible ("treasure for 20eur").
5. Able-person outcomes dominate: better relationships, clearer thinking, parenting confidence, zest for life, "more capable."

Our SA wedge (R400 Instant EFT + emotional/self-mastery ranking pages) should mirror this psychology on non-YMYL language.

## Conversion language patterns (English, usable)

### A. Pre-purchase / curiosity
- "Ordered, I am curious whether it will help me find answers and overcome a difficult period."
- "Whoever wants to know where their life is stuck, let them buy — here they will get answers on what is holding them back."
- "I'm looking forward to reading it."
- "Just need to find the time to read it — I think it will be very important to understand what is written in it."

### B. Mechanism / understanding
- "Only here did I find the whole puzzle put together. I found answers about how the human mind works… and most importantly, there is also a solution."
- "It answered many questions that had troubled me since childhood."
- "Helped me understand myself and why some unwanted situations happen to me in life and the solution to them."
- "Why people act unpredictably… why I have stress or fear even when there is no reason… why relationships fall apart."

### C. Able-person outcomes (priority for SA ranking pages)
- "I understand myself and my surroundings much better, and I have a greater zest for life."
- "Thanks to it, I am more capable and happier."
- "Better relationships and a happier life."
- "My daughter is much wiser… she already applies what she found… understands life and people better."
- "I know why they do what they do, I don't need to guess anything."

### D. Life-change proof (use sparingly, as social proof not medical claim)
- "This book changed my life for the better."
- "I really managed to change my life."
- "Her life changed… after reading it."
- "Changed my life from the ground up."

### E. Recommend / low-pressure CTA
- "Let everyone decide for themselves, I am not here to persuade anyone."
- "Getting a treasure in the form of understanding for 20eur? That's a super deal."
- "Definitely recommend reading and applying."
- "Mandatory reading for every parent and every manager!" (SK voice — adapt; avoid "disorders")

## Able-person filter signals in real buyers

From the testimonials, buyers who stayed engaged show:

- Interest in **understanding** (not only relief)
- Willingness to **apply** ("reading and applying")
- Rereading / studying (tool users)
- Outcomes framed as **more able**: clearer, better relationships, better parenting, zest, capability
- Low persuasion dependence ("decide for yourself")

**Not prominent:** permanent victim language, pure sympathy-seeking, "fix me because I can't."

This matches operator rule in doc 06: write for the dissatisfied but capable.

## What NOT to copy onto SA ranking pages

1. Clinical framing ("solve psychological disorders") — YMYL risk.
2. Guarantees of curing illness or marriage outcomes as claims.
3. Trademark-heavy Hubbard/Dianetics branding in H1s for ranking pages (use problem language; book as conversion end).
4. Wholesale Slovak testimonial text (copyright + authenticity; create SA-native proof later).

---

## Implications for page + funnel design

1. **Ranking pages:** lead with problem language from HIGH SERP list (doc 12), not "buy Dianetics."
2. **Bridge section:** mechanism in plain language (stored stress reactions / reactive patterns) — public Dianetics framing from doc 05.
3. **Proof pattern:** "answers + something you can apply" > abstract wellness.
4. **CTA:** Instant EFT R400 + "decide for yourself" tone (low pressure, high respect).
5. **SA-specific proof to build:** workplace/trapped-stress and high-performer emptiness are under-served vs SK relationship proof — own them.
6. **Testimonial UI:** Facebook-style raw comments convert; when we have SA buyers, mirror that format.
